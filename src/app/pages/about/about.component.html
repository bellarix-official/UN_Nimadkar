Firm Overview

Technical Precision with Practical Business Understanding

U N Nimbadkar & Associates is an Ahmedabad-based boutique Cost & Management Accounting practice founded by CMA Urvesh Nimbadkar (Associate Cost & Management Accountant).

We deliver robust financial frameworks, statutory audits, and management consultancy to emerging startups, MSMEs, LLPs, and corporate entities across India.

We believe every enterprise deserves access to world-class financial leadership, rigorous internal controls, and deep costing insights that drive sustainable profitability. Our practice bridges the gap between everyday accounting data entry and executive management direction.
