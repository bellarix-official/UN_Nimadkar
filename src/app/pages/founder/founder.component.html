Founder's Direction & Leadership

Bridging Shop-Floor Costing with Boardroom Decision Making

CMA Urvesh Nimbadkar is a practicing Cost and Management Accountant and Associate Member of the Institute of Cost Accountants of India (ICMAI). He leads U N Nimbadkar & Associates with a clear mandate: making complex costing and statutory compliance understandable, practical, and highly value-accretive for enterprise leaders.

Rather than treating accounting as a passive year-end tax compliance exercise, Urvesh works hands-on with business owners, factory heads, and finance controllers. He helps design leak-proof Bills of Materials (BOM), formulate Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), establish departmental budgets, and safeguard margins against volatile market shifts.

Founder's Core Philosophy:

"Most growing companies do not struggle because of lack of sales; they struggle because of unseen cost leakages on the factory floor and delayed financial visibility. Our commitment is to give entrepreneurs total command over their numbers."
